About me

Engineer by training. Builder by habit.

I started my career as a software developer at a multinational engineering company in India, where I spent four years building production software on a professional engineering team. After moving to the USA, I kept my skills sharp the only way that really works — by building real things and shipping them.

I taught myself WordPress and built client websites. Then I learned Android Studio and shipped an Android app. To reach iPhone users I picked up Xcode and Swift. Finally I rebuilt everything in Flutter, so one codebase now serves both platforms — faster to build, cheaper for clients, identical on iOS and Android.

Today I take projects from first idea to live launch on my own — design, development, app store submission, and everything in between. IntelliEats, which I developed from my daughter's award-winning science fair idea, is live on both the App Store and Google Play. I also have five books published on Amazon and two more apps in development.

I also build websites to be found by AI agents, not just Google. As more people ask ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity for recommendations instead of searching, I make sure a site is structured so those systems can read it, understand it, and cite it.

Alongside my work, I've spent the last five years as a parent coach and volunteer in elementary, middle, and high schools — coaching Science Olympiad, mentoring science fair students, leading robotics and coding sessions, and serving as room parent. Teaching my own son math at home took him to 1st place nationally in Math Kangaroo, and the science fair project I mentored placed 1st at SEFMD. We've since published those methods as the Quick Math Tricks workbook series on Amazon, co-authored together. It's proof that the material I create actually works — and it taught me to explain technical ideas simply, which is exactly what clients want.

Common questions

Good to know before we start

Do you build apps for both iPhone and Android?

Yes. I build in Flutter, which means one codebase runs on both iOS and Android. It's faster to build and costs less than commissioning two separate apps, and the experience stays consistent across platforms. I also handle App Store and Google Play submission for you.

What is AI crawl optimization, and do I need it?

More and more people ask ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Perplexity for recommendations instead of searching Google. Those AI systems can only recommend businesses whose websites they can properly read and understand. AI crawl optimization — also called Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) or LLM Optimization — structures your site with schema markup, machine-readable content and correct crawler rules so you get cited in those answers instead of skipped. If your customers could plausibly ask an AI "who should I hire for this?", then yes, you need it.

How much does a website or app cost?

Every project is different, so I quote each one after a short conversation about what you actually need. You'll get a clear, fixed price and timeline before any work starts — no hourly surprises and no scope creep charges.

What's your experience?

I spent four years as a software developer at a multinational engineering company in India before moving to the USA. Since then I've shipped over ten live projects on my own: IntelliEats (rated 5.0★ on the App Store and live on Google Play), four websites, and five books published on Amazon. You can try all of them from the work section above.

How long will my project take?

A straightforward website is usually a couple of weeks; an app depends heavily on the features you need. I'll give you a realistic timeline in the quote, and I share progress as I go so you're never wondering where things stand.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. I'm based in Michigan, USA and work with clients anywhere. Most projects run over email and WhatsApp, with calls whenever they're useful.
